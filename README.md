## About Astera

Astera is a German innovator in wireless LED lighting solutions, engineered for film, TV, events, and creative applications. Since 2005, Astera has revolutionized the industry with 100% wireless, battery-powered fixtures that combine cutting-edge technology with unmatched reliability and ease of use.

## Core Technologies

### Wireless LED Innovation
- 100% wireless operation with integrated batteries
- Built-in wireless DMX receivers (CRMX/W-DMX compatible)
- AsteraApp for intuitive smartphone/tablet control
- No cables required for power or data

### German Engineering
- Designed and engineered in Germany
- Rugged, weatherproof construction (IP65/IP55 rated)
- Professional-grade components and reliability
- Innovative thermal management systems
